### Comprehensive Concept Modules

#### 1. ML Foundations, Types & Problem Formulations

- **Target Folder:** `ML/01_ml_foundations_problems/`
- **Core Topics:** Supervised vs. Unsupervised vs. Semi-Supervised vs. Self-Supervised vs. Reinforcement Learning; Classification, Regression, Clustering, Ranking, Anomaly Detection; Parametric vs. Non-Parametric algorithms; Batch Learning vs. Online/Incremental Learning; Inductive Bias; Offline evaluation vs. Production feedback loops.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Identifying problem types from ambiguous business goals, choosing batch vs. streaming algorithms, framing recommendation problems as collaborative filtering vs. ranking.

---

#### 2. Data Preprocessing, Imputation & Scaling

- **Target Folder:** `ML/02_data_preprocessing_scaling/`
- **Core Topics:** Missing data mechanisms (MCAR, MAR, MNAR); Univariate imputation (Mean, Median, Mode) vs. Multivariate imputation (`KNNImputer`, `IterativeImputer`); Outlier detection & winsorization (Z-score, IQR fence rules); Feature scaling (`StandardScaler`, `MinMaxScaler`, `RobustScaler`, `MaxAbsScaler`); Power transformations (Log, Box-Cox, Yeo-Johnson).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Choosing `RobustScaler` over `StandardScaler` for heavy-tailed financial data, fitting scalers strictly on training folds to prevent data leakage, handling zeroes during log transforms.

---

#### 3. Feature Engineering & Encodings

- **Target Folder:** `ML/03_feature_engineering_encodings/`
- **Core Topics:** High-cardinality categorical encoding (One-Hot, Ordinal, Target/Mean Encoding with smoothing, Frequency/Count, Weight of Evidence); Numerical feature crosses and polynomial interactions; Cyclical temporal encoding ($\sin/\cos$ transformations for hours/months); Discretization & Binning (`KBinsDiscretizer`); Text feature extraction (`CountVectorizer`, `TfidfVectorizer`, n-grams).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Preventing target leakage during out-of-fold target encoding, handling unseen categories in production inference, mitigating curse of dimensionality from One-Hot Encoding.

---

#### 4. Feature Selection & Dimensionality Reduction (PCA)

- **Target Folder:** `ML/04_feature_selection_pca/`
- **Core Topics:** Filter methods (Pearson correlation, Variance Threshold, Chi-Square $\chi^2$, ANOVA $F$-test, Mutual Information); Wrapper methods (Forward Selection, Backward Elimination, Recursive Feature Elimination / RFE); Embedded methods (L1 sparsity, tree feature importances); Principal Component Analysis (Covariance matrix, Eigenvalues/Eigenvectors, Explained Variance Ratio, scree plots); Truncated SVD, t-SNE perplexity, and UMAP.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Determining optimal PCA components for 95% variance retention, understanding why t-SNE is strictly a visualization tool and cannot transform new test points, debugging collinear feature elimination.

---

#### 5. Dataset Splitting, Cross-Validation & Data Leakage

- **Target Folder:** `ML/05_validation_leakage_cv/`
- **Core Topics:** Holdout validation vs. K-Fold Cross-Validation; Stratified K-Fold for class imbalance; Repeated K-Fold; Group K-Fold for clustered/grouped samples; Time-Series Split (rolling origin, expanding window); Data Leakage taxonomies (target leakage, train-test contamination during scaling/encoding, temporal lookahead leakage).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Identifying subtle data leakage in cross-validation pipelines (e.g., executing `fit_transform` before splitting), choosing Group K-Fold for multi-visit patient clinical data, designing leakage-free temporal CV splits.

---

#### 6. Linear & Polynomial Regression

- **Target Folder:** `ML/06_linear_polynomial_regression/` _(Existing: [`linear test prompt.md`](file:///d:/Data/GitHub/AI-ML-Study-Notes/ML/linear%20test%20prompt.md))_
- **Core Topics:** Simple & Multiple Linear Regression; Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) closed-form Normal Equation: $\beta = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T y$; Gauss-Markov Theorem; Regression assumptions: Linearity, Independence, Homoscedasticity, Normality of Residuals, No Multicollinearity; Variance Inflation Factor (VIF); Polynomial Regression; Evaluation metrics: MAE, MSE, RMSE, RMSLE, $R^2$, Adjusted $R^2$, MAPE; Residual diagnostic plots (Q-Q plots, Residuals vs. Fitted).

---

#### 7. Regularization & Bias-Variance Tradeoff

- **Target Folder:** `ML/07_regularization_ridge_lasso/` _(Existing: [`regularization test prompt.md`](file:///d:/Data/GitHub/AI-ML-Study-Notes/ML/regularization%20test%20prompt.md))_
- **Core Topics:** Mathematical bias-variance decomposition; Overfitting vs. Underfitting; Ridge Regression ($L_2$ penalty $\lambda \sum \beta_j^2$, analytical solution $(X^T X + \lambda I)^{-1} X^T y$, weight shrinkage); Lasso Regression ($L_1$ penalty $\lambda \sum |\beta_j|$, subgradient optimization, sparsity & feature elimination); Elastic Net ($L_1 + L_2$ convex combination, $\alpha$ and $l_1\text{-ratio}$); Effect of feature scaling on regularization penalties.

---

#### 8. Logistic Regression & Classification Foundations

- **Target Folder:** `ML/08_logistic_regression/` _(Existing: [`logistic test prompt.md`](file:///d:/Data/GitHub/AI-ML-Study-Notes/ML/logistic%20test%20prompt.md))_
- **Core Topics:** Sigmoid activation function $\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$; Odds ratio and Log-Odds (Logit) transformation; Binary Cross-Entropy / Log-Loss objective; Gradient descent updates; Decision boundaries and threshold selection; Multiclass classification architectures: One-vs-Rest (OvR) vs. Multinomial / Softmax regression; Regularization parameter $C = 1/\lambda$.

---

#### 9. K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

- **Target Folder:** `ML/09_k_nearest_neighbors/`
- **Core Topics:** Instance-based / Lazy learning paradigm; Distance metrics: Euclidean, Manhattan ($L_1$), Minkowski ($L_p$), Cosine distance, Hamming distance; Choosing optimal $K$ (low $K$ overfitting vs. high $K$ underfitting); Uniform vs. Distance-weighted voting ($w = 1/d$); Algorithmic data structures: Brute force, KD-Tree, Ball-Tree; Curse of dimensionality impact on nearest neighbors.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Explaining why feature scaling is mandatory before KNN, predicting boundary changes as $K \to N$, handling tie-breaking in even $K$ scenarios.

---

#### 10. Naive Bayes Classifiers

- **Target Folder:** `ML/10_naive_bayes/`
- **Core Topics:** Bayes' Theorem: $P(y|X) = \frac{P(X|y) P(y)}{P(X)}$; The Conditional Independence Assumption; Gaussian Naive Bayes (continuous features with normal likelihood); Multinomial Naive Bayes (integer counts, text word frequencies); Bernoulli Naive Bayes (binary indicator features); Zero-frequency probability problem; Laplace ($\alpha=1$) and Lidstone ($\alpha < 1$) smoothing.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Calculating posterior probabilities by hand given class priors and conditional likelihood tables, explaining why Naive Bayes outputs poorly calibrated probability estimates despite strong ranking accuracy.

---

#### 11. Support Vector Machines (SVM)

- **Target Folder:** `ML/11_support_vector_machines/`
- **Core Topics:** Optimal separating hyperplane; Support vectors and margin geometry ($2/\|w\|$); Hard-margin vs. Soft-margin SVM; Slack variables ($\xi_i$) and the regularization parameter $C$; The Kernel Trick (Mercer's theorem); Kernel functions: Linear, Polynomial, Radial Basis Function (RBF/Gaussian), Sigmoid; RBF parameter $\gamma$ (gamma) impact on decision boundaries; Support Vector Regression (SVR) with $\varepsilon$-insensitive loss tube.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Diagnosing overfitting caused by high $C$ and high $\gamma$, computational complexity scaling $O(N^2 \text{ to } N^3)$ with sample size, dual vs. primal optimization formulations.

---

#### 12. Decision Trees (Classification & Regression)

- **Target Folder:** `ML/12_decision_trees/`
- **Core Topics:** Tree structure (root, internal splits, leaf nodes); Orthogonal axis-aligned decision boundaries; Splitting criteria: Entropy, Information Gain (ID3), Gini Impurity (CART), Variance Reduction (Regression Trees); Overfitting controls: `max_depth`, `min_samples_split`, `min_samples_leaf`, `max_features`; Pre-pruning vs. Cost-Complexity Post-Pruning (minimal cost-complexity parameter $\alpha$); Instability to small data perturbations.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Calculating Gini impurity and Information Gain manually for a candidate split, diagnosing why decision trees cannot extrapolate beyond training range bounds in regression.

---

#### 13. Ensemble Learning: Bagging & Random Forests

- **Target Folder:** `ML/13_ensemble_bagging_random_forest/`
- **Core Topics:** Ensemble principles (variance reduction, wisdom of crowds); Bootstrap Aggregation (Bagging with replacement); Out-of-Bag (OOB) sample evaluation (approximating 36.8% unselected samples); Random Forest algorithm: bootstrap sampling + random feature subspace selection at each split (`max_features = sqrt(p)`); Extremely Randomized Trees (Extra Trees); Feature importances: Mean Decrease in Impurity (MDI) vs. Permutation Importance.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Evaluating OOB score as a validation substitute, explaining why MDI biases toward high-cardinality features and how permutation importance corrects this.

---

#### 14. Ensemble Learning: Boosting Algorithms

- **Target Folder:** `ML/14_ensemble_boosting_algorithms/`
- **Core Topics:** Boosting mechanics (sequential learning, converting weak learners to strong models, bias reduction); AdaBoost (sample weight updates, learner voting weights $\alpha_m$); Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM: fitting trees to pseudo-residuals / negative gradients, shrinkage/learning rate $\eta$); XGBoost (exact vs. approximate greedy splits, second-order Taylor expansion gradients $g_i$ and Hessians $h_i$, regularization $\lambda, \gamma$); LightGBM (Histogram binning, GOSS, EFB, Leaf-wise growth); CatBoost (ordered boosting, symmetric oblivious trees).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Tuning learning rate vs. number of estimators, identifying gradient vanishing/exploding in tree residuals, choosing between leaf-wise and depth-wise tree growth.

---

#### 15. Ensemble Learning: Voting, Stacking & Blending

- **Target Folder:** `ML/15_ensemble_voting_stacking/`
- **Core Topics:** Majority Hard Voting vs. Probability-weighted Soft Voting; Stacking (Stacked Generalization): Base models (Level 0) and Meta-model / Blender (Level 1); Out-of-Fold (OOF) prediction generation to train the meta-learner without data leakage; Blending using a single holdout validation set; Ensemble diversity principle (combining structurally dissimilar model families).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Designing a leak-free 5-fold stacking pipeline in scikit-learn (`StackingClassifier`), diagnosing multi-collinearity among Level-0 predictions passed to a linear meta-learner.

---

#### 16. Classification Evaluation & Imbalanced Data

- **Target Folder:** `ML/16_classification_evaluation_imbalance/`
- **Core Topics:** Confusion Matrix (TP, TN, FP, FN, Type I vs. Type II errors); Accuracy and the Accuracy Paradox; Precision, Recall / Sensitivity, Specificity, $F_1\text{-Score}$, $F_\beta\text{-Score}$; ROC Curve, Area Under the Curve (ROC-AUC); Precision-Recall Curve (PR-AUC); Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC); Cohen's Kappa; Threshold tuning; Imbalanced learning techniques: Class weights (`class_weight='balanced'`), Random Undersampling, Random Oversampling, SMOTE, ADASYN.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Choosing PR-AUC over ROC-AUC for severe class imbalances (e.g., 0.1% fraud detection), selecting $F_2$ when false negatives are critical (medical diagnostics), tuning decision thresholds for cost-asymmetric matrices.

---

#### 17. Unsupervised Learning: Clustering

- **Target Folder:** `ML/17_unsupervised_clustering/`
- **Core Topics:** K-Means Clustering: Objective function (Inertia / Within-Cluster Sum of Squares - WCSS), Lloyd's algorithm, K-Means++ initialization; Determining $K$: The Elbow Method, Silhouette Analysis (Silhouette Coefficient $[-1, 1]$), Davies-Bouldin Index; Hierarchical Agglomerative Clustering: Linkage methods (Single, Complete, Average, Ward), Dendrogram cutting; Density-based DBSCAN ($\varepsilon$, `min_samples`, core/border/noise points); Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) with Expectation-Maximization (EM).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Diagnosing K-Means failures on non-spherical or irregular clusters, tuning DBSCAN $\varepsilon$ using $k$-distance elbow plots, evaluating soft cluster probabilities in GMMs.

---

#### 18. Anomaly & Outlier Detection

- **Target Folder:** `ML/18_anomaly_outlier_detection/`
- **Core Topics:** Taxonomies: Point anomalies, Contextual anomalies, Collective anomalies; Statistical methods (Z-score, IQR fences, Elliptic Envelope / Mahalanobis distance); Distance & Density-based: Local Outlier Factor (LOF); Tree-based: Isolation Forest (isolation depth in random recursive partitioning); Boundary-based: One-Class SVM (OC-SVM); Autoencoders for anomaly detection; Semi-supervised vs. Unsupervised anomaly detection.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Setting contamination rates in `IsolationForest`, detecting anomalies in high-dimensional tabular data, evaluating anomaly models using Precision@K and PR-AUC.

---

#### 19. Model Optimization, Gradient Descent & Hyperparameter Tuning

- **Target Folder:** `ML/19_optimization_hyperparameter_tuning/`
- **Core Topics:** Mathematical optimization: Convex vs. Non-Convex functions, Global vs. Local minima, Saddle points; Gradient Descent variants: Batch Gradient Descent, Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), Mini-Batch Gradient Descent; Learning rates, Momentum, and Learning rate decay; Hyperparameter search strategies: Grid Search CV, Random Search CV, Halving Grid Search; Bayesian Optimization (Surrogate models, Gaussian Processes, TPE via Optuna, Expected Improvement acquisition).
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Detecting exploding or vanishing gradients from learning rate choices, comparing computational budgets between Grid Search and Bayesian Optimization, diagnosing hyperparameter overfitting on validation sets.

---

#### 20. Model Interpretability & Explainable AI (XAI)

- **Target Folder:** `ML/20_explainable_ai_interpretability/`
- **Core Topics:** Global vs. Local interpretability; Inherently interpretable models (Linear models, shallow Decision Trees, GAMs) vs. Post-hoc explainers; Permutation Feature Importance (PFI); Partial Dependence Plots (PDP) & Individual Conditional Expectation (ICE) plots; SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations): Cooperative game theory axioms (Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy, Additivity), TreeSHAP vs. KernelSHAP, Beeswarm plots, Force plots; LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) local surrogate models.
- **Diagnostics & Scenarios:** Detecting correlated feature distortions in Permutation Importance, interpreting positive and negative SHAP feature contributions for a loan denial, explaining model predictions to non-technical stakeholders.
